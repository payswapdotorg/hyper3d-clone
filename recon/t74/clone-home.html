Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers — and more.

What makes Hyper3D useful for 3D generation?+

It turns text prompts or reference images into 3D assets with clean topology and per-part PBR materials. Previews are free, redos are free, and a standard confirm costs 1 credit — agentic runs 10 — so drafting costs nothing until you keep a result.

What kind of image works best with Hyper3D?+

For image-to-3D, one reference image of the subject is enough. Resolution, framing, background, and lighting are capture choices you can tune for clarity — they shape the result, they are not submission requirements.

Can I optionally use more than one image?+

Yes, optionally. Rodin works from a single image without a prescribed pose or camera angle; additional views simply add visual guidance for geometry and texture when you have them.

What can I export from Hyper3D?+

The six formats this codebase really writes: GLB (Web & AR), GLTF (Web & AR), FBX (Unity & Unreal), OBJ (Editing), STL (Printing), USDZ (Apple AR). The exporters run locally in the studio — what you download is the mesh you previewed.

Can Hyper3D models be used in commercial projects?+

That is a licensing question, so it has a legal answer rather than a marketing one: the terms of use page states what this clone grants, and every export you confirm is yours to use within those terms.

How can I improve the quality of my generated 3D model?+

Three honest levers: raise the effort tier (Extreme-Low ~4s to Extreme-High ~80s trades time for fidelity), give the clearest reference you have, and add extra views when available. Redos are free, so comparing tiers costs nothing.

Is Hyper3D only for artists, or can developers use it too?+

Both. Artists get the studio surface — prompt, constrain, preview, export. Developers get the documented API surface (generation, task state, exports) on the API feature page, honest about which routes run here.

What can I do after generating a model with Hyper3D?+

Export it for the workflow you built it for — printing (STL), engines (FBX), web and AR (GLB/GLTF), Apple AR (USDZ), or general editing (OBJ) — or re-run it free until the draft is worth confirming.
